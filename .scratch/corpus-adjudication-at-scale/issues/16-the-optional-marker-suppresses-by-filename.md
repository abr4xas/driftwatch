# 16: `optional` is a third of the `hedged` gate, and it reaches across sentences

**What to decide:** whether the bare `optional` entry in `HEDGED` earns its place, given that
it fires more than any other marker in the list and defends nothing the corpus has measured.

**Type:** task

**Blocked by:** nothing — `07` produced the evidence

**Status:** open

## What `07` measured, over 700 repositories

[`context-prose.ts`](../../../src/extract/context-prose.ts)'s `HEDGED` markers are tested
with `includes` against the **two-line window**, and the list holds `'optional'` as a bare
substring next to the narrower `'(optional)'`. The `hedged` gate refused 266 candidates and

> **`optional` accounts for 87 of them — a third of the gate — and 33 are not known to be
> satisfied.** The next marker down fires 32 times.

`(optional)`, the parenthesised form the entry was presumably meant to cover, fires 4 times.

## The shape, stated accurately

The interesting cases are not one thing, and an earlier draft of this ticket got the
proportion wrong. Of the 33:

- **One** is silenced because the marker is inside the **filename**: `core/optional.yaml`.
  That is the memorable case and it is a single discard.
- **Several** are silenced because the word sits in a *neighbouring sentence* of the same
  two-line window. `haddocking/haddock3`:

  ```
  Mandatory top-level parameters (`run_dir`, `molecules`) are defined in `core/mandatory.yaml`.
  Optional top-level parameters (`preprocess`, `postprocess`, `gen_archive`) are in
  `core/optional.yaml`. Global execution parameters are in `modules/defaults.yaml`.
  ```

  Three files asserted as plainly as a document can, all three silenced, and `Optional` is
  qualifying *parameters* — it is not hedging about any of them.
- **Roughly half are the marker working.** "Optional: existing `design/concept.md` (if user
  is iterating on a draft)" is exactly what the entry is for.

So the defect is **scope**, not the word: `optional` is broad enough that a two-line window
carries it into claims it has nothing to do with. That is the same failure
`opensItsOwnItem` was written for — the `e.g.` in one table row suppressing the next — and
the same reason `CONDITIONAL` is tested against the sentence rather than the window.

The other markers behave. `is generated`, `are generated`, `generated by`, `generated from`,
`auto-generated` and `autogenerated` account for ~90 discards between them and only ~25 name an
absent path, which is what a marker about files a command writes should look like.

## What it defends: nothing that has been measured

Removing the bare `'optional'` entry and leaving `'(optional)'` was run against the
certification corpus:

```
66 repos · 320 sources · 33 findings
  calibration: 21 · validation: 12
```

**Identical**, finding for finding. `CLASSIFICATION.md` names no false positive class it
prevents and does not contain the string. By the criterion `07` § "The trap to avoid" sets
out — a high rate of real-looking discards *and* no false-positive class it is defending —
this is the entry in the file that comes closest to meeting both halves.

## The options

1. **Test `optional` against the sentence, not the window.** Follows `CONDITIONAL`'s
   precedent for the same reason — it is a broad word and the window carries it into a
   neighbour. Keeps the half of the 33 that are real hedges, drops the `haddock3` shape.
   The most faithful to what was measured, and the most code.
2. **Drop `'optional'`, keep `'(optional)'`.** Measured above: nothing moves in the 66. Also
   drops the real hedges — "Optional: `design/concept.md`" would start being reported, and
   that is a false positive nobody has counted.
3. **Leave it.** 33 suppressed claims in 700 repositories, against `AGENTS.md`'s rule that one
   false positive costs more than ten false negatives. Defensible.

Option 1 looks right. Option 2 is the one that was measured, and the measurement does not
cover what it would cost — the 66 do not contain the shape at all, which is why nothing moved.

Whichever is taken it is a change to `src/extract/`, so `AGENTS.md` § "The corpus is a local
gate" requires the corpus before and after with the diff read by hand.

## What is not in question

The other entries in `HEDGED`. `07` looked at every `hedged` discard's marker and this is the
only one whose *scope* is wrong rather than merely costly.
